Frame 1
Auditable, not just Agentic
✦ Trust
Your agent explains itself.
Every time.
📍 Scene
GM on the go — mobile view. GM opens the Action Detail screen for a rate change made at 2am. She sees the full decision trail: demand spike detected → comp set checked → rate increased €155 → booking confirmed 4 min later.
GMs don't just want automation — they want to be able to stand behind every decision. The agent doesn't just act, it leaves a complete audit trail. Ask it anything, replay any decision, export the logic for your owner meeting.
GM Quote "Why did we sell the Penthouse for €200 last night?" — Agent replays the full logic in the chat, in plain English.
Frame 2
Report on Strategy, not just Performance
📊 Reporting
Numbers tell what happened.
Strategy explains why.
📍 Scene
GM is in an owner meeting in Zoho. GM opens the Owner Report — it shows the strategies taken this week: "ADR up 12% because comp set checked daily, which drove bookings from the leisure segment 4 and drove the strategy decision to show better results and improve the experience."
Traditional PMS reports show revenue and occupancy. The Performance Agent reports on decisions — what was done, why, and what it produced. Your owner gets context, not just charts.
GM Quote "Finally a report I can present to my owner without spending Sunday afternoon on it."
Frame 3
Mobile = the Key Win for Indies
📱 Mobile First
Run your hotel from your phone.
Seriously.
📍 Scene
Five-star hotel, multi-duty agent stories. Notifications arrive: "Agente — overnight summary — €41,459 generated, 5 actions taken, 2 decisions waiting for you." GM swipes — lands directly on the Agent Feed with the 2 pending decisions at the top.
Independent hoteliers are never at their desk. They're at the front desk, in the kitchen, with a guest. The Performance Agent pushes the right information to their phone — no login, no dashboard hunting. The key decisions surface automatically.
GM Quote "I approved a rate change from the school pickup line. That's what I needed."
Frame 4
Information Pushed, Not Pulled
⚡ Push Intelligence
The right information finds you.
Not the other way around.
📍 Scene
Mobile lock screen, 7:14am. Phone notification: "Agent overnight summary — €41,459 generated, 5 actions taken, 2 decisions waiting for you." GM swipes — lands directly on the Agent Feed with the 2 pending decisions at the top.
Most hotel software requires GMs to log in and go looking. The Performance Agent inverts this. It knows what's urgent, what's informational, and what can stay invisible. Your attention is protected, not overwhelmed.

Frame 5
Radically Personalised Reporting
✦ Personalisation
Your dashboard. Your language.
Your metrics.
📍 Scene
Desktop dashboard. GM types in chat: "Show me RevPAR by channel for the last 30 days." A chart appears in chat. Below it: "Save to dashboard?" GM clicks Yes. The chart pins to dashboard. She drags it next to occupancy widget, removes the ADR card she never uses.
No two GMs run their hotel the same way. The Performance Agent adapts to how you think — not the other way around. Build the view that matches your brain, not the one a product manager designed for an average user.
GM Quote "I moved the RevPAR widget next to comp set. Took 3 seconds. Never going back."
Frame 6
The Kill Switch
🚨 Crisis Control
You're always in control.
One button, zero doubt.
📍 Scene
Fire alarm going off at the hotel, the front-desk manager has just handed in their resignation, and the owner is talking about reflagging the 3rd floor and they just announced Taylor Swift concert in 2 minutes. GM feels overwhelmed.
Agentic software that can't be stopped is a liability. The Kill Switch gives GMs total confidence: the moment something feels wrong — an error, a system error, a gut feeling — they can freeze all autonomous actions instantly, across mobile and desktop, with a single deliberate gesture.
GM Quote "During our fire alarm last month, I paused the agent in 2 seconds. No rate changes while the hotel was in chaos. That's what trust looks like."
